Guidelines
Rules & Submission
01Continuous 24-hour development on-site only. Core logic must be developed during the event.
02Team size: 2–4 members. Interdisciplinary teams are highly encouraged.
03Bring Your Own Problem (BYOP) aligned with a domain, or choose predefined tasks.
04Required Submissions: Source Code (GitHub), Presentation (PPT/PDF), and Live Demo.
05Plagiarism or use of fully pre-built projects leads to immediate disqualification.
06Prohibited: Copying code, tampering with other teams' work, or any unethical activity.
07Code of Conduct: No harassment or discrimination. Participants must bring their own hardware.
08Final presentations: 5–7 minutes demo followed by 2–3 minutes Q&A with the judging panel.
